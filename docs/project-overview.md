# PROJECT DHWANI 2.4 — Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

PROJECT DHWANI 2.4, titled *The Panchatattva Acoustic Defence Grid*, is a multi-terrain acoustic monitoring research initiative exploring the integration of distributed sensing, signal processing, embedded intelligence, and edge artificial intelligence.

The project investigates how acoustic, seismic, and thermal sensing concepts can contribute to environmental monitoring in geographically challenging conditions.

## 2. Problem Statement

Conventional visual monitoring approaches can face limitations in environments affected by:

- Dense vegetation and difficult terrain.
- Fog, rain, and changing weather conditions.
- Remote geographical locations.
- Limited observation infrastructure.
- Complex environmental signal conditions.

DHWANI explores complementary sensing approaches to address these monitoring challenges.

## 3. Project Objectives

The major research objectives are:

1. Explore multi-modal environmental sensing.
2. Investigate acoustic and seismic signal interpretation.
3. Study AI-assisted signal classification.
4. Explore edge-based intelligent processing.
5. Develop a modular and adaptable monitoring architecture.
6. Establish a pathway for controlled testing and validation.

## 5. Proposed System Components

### 5.1 Hawk-2

A conceptual aerial observation platform within the overall system architecture.

### 5.2 Panchatattva

Five terrain-specific sensing concepts:

- Maru — Desert
- Hima — Mountain
- Nadi — Riverine
- Vana — Jungle
- Sagara — Maritime

### 5.3 Fence Sentinel

A conceptual vibration-monitoring subsystem.

### 5.4 AI Brain

An intelligent processing architecture exploring signal interpretation and multi-modal information analysis.

### 5.5 Mesh Communication

A distributed communication concept for information exchange between system components.
